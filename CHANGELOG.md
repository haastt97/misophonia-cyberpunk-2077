# Changelog

## 0.3.9

### Added

- **Body sounds → Player Breathing and Panting** covers V’s locomotion breathing and low-stamina panting.
- **Voice sounds → Ambient NPC Phone Calls** covers generic crowd and metro phone conversations.
- **Environmental sounds → Mud and Wet Movement** covers the wet crawl and mud impacts in the landfill sequence.

### Fixed

- **Breathing** covers Jackie’s fast and slow breathing after the balcony jump in **The Heist**.
- **Retching and Vomiting** covers another scripted vomiting event.
- Global **All** synchronizes the **Screens** slider with the value used by the audio mixer, including when settings are still initializing.

The **Body Sounds**, **Environmental Sounds**, **Voice Sounds**, and **All Covered Sounds** standalone archives include the corresponding new events.

## 0.3.8

- Added 23 book, newspaper, and paper-handling events under **Repetitive sounds → Paper and Plastic Rustling**.
- Added those events to the **Repetitive Sounds** and **All Covered Sounds** standalone archives.

## 0.3.7

- Moved runtime controls to redscript and removed the Cyber Engine Tweaks requirement.
- Added eight standalone archive options for fixed mutes.

## 0.3.6

- Added an **All** switch to each of the six sound sections.
